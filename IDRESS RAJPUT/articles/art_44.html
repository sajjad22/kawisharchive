The First line of defense, when the river is in floods requires close and constant patrol and unremitting supervision, both by day and right, by adequate, trained staff a stitch in time saves nine. Timely warning and timely action, witch efficient, unremitting patrolling alone can provide, will save a dangerous situation while complacency born of a false sense of security following a series of low rivers, may lead to disaster. Continuous vigilance in patrolling every where is, therefore, enjoined on all the staff, particularly during the night and in the early hours of morning, when branches most frequently occur with the slackening of supervision.
